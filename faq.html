Common Questions from Founders and Business Owners

The Business Health Checkup is a confidential diagnostic evaluation assessing core operational and financial indicators across six business pillars: Financial Health, Sales Pipeline, Marketing & Brand, Operations, IT & Digital Systems, and Governance & Legal Protection. It generates an indicative score (0–100) and an executive preliminary report highlighting your company's primary strengths, risk areas, and immediate priorities.

The Checkup is an initial high-level triage based on survey responses and preliminary indicators to identify whether and where vulnerabilities exist. The Diagnostic is an intensive forensic investigation where our senior consultants examine your actual books of accounts, ledger entries, debtor cycles, customer concentration, and operational workflows to isolate root causes and prescribe a prioritized 30/60/90-day turnaround roadmap.

For the initial online checkup, you provide a brief overview of your business or upload financial summaries (e.g. Trial Balance, P&L summary, or debtor aging in PDF/XLSX/CSV format up to 4 MB). Sensitive identification numbers like PAN or Aadhaar are automatically redacted before analysis by our secure DPDP-compliant pipeline.

Confidentiality is fundamental to our practice. Uploaded documents are processed in-memory during assessment and are not stored permanently in public buckets. We adhere to strict non-disclosure obligations, and only certified Chartered Accountants and authorized consultants have access to your numbers. Your business data is never shared with third parties or advertisers.
