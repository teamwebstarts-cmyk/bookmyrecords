Business Health Checkup

Understand the Health of Your Business Before You Plan Its Next Stage of Growth

A business can appear active and profitable while important issues remain hidden in its finances, operations or compliance. BookMyRecords' Business Health Checkup helps business owners step back, review the key areas of their enterprise and understand what is working well, what needs attention and where improvement may be possible.

Designed for startups, MSMEs and growing businesses, this review brings important business information into a clearer picture. It helps you make informed decisions, prioritise the right areas and build a stronger foundation for sustainable growth.

A Clearer View of Where Your Business Stands

  • A Whole-Business Perspective: Review key business functions together instead of looking at financial or operational information in isolation.
  • Early Visibility into Key Gaps: Bring potential inefficiencies, control gaps and areas requiring closer review to the surface.
  • Practical Priorities for Owners: Turn observations into a focused set of next steps based on business needs, available information and priorities.

What We Review During Your Business Health Checkup

  • • Identification of apparent documentation or process gaps

Frequently Asked Questions on Business Health Checkup

Practical answers on scope, documents, and how the checkup differs from a statutory audit or deeper diagnostic.

A Business Health Checkup is a structured, high-level review of important areas of a business, such as financial performance, accounting practices, tax compliance, operations and management reporting. It helps owners understand the current position and identify areas that may need attention.

It can be useful for startups, proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, private limited companies and MSMEs that want a clearer view of their business position. It is particularly relevant before a period of expansion, investment, restructuring or significant business change.

Depending on the agreed scope, information may include financial statements, trial balance, bank statements, sales and purchase summaries, GST and TDS filing records, receivables and payables data, and basic operational information. The exact list is shared after understanding the business.

No. A Business Health Checkup is an advisory review intended to highlight observations and improvement areas. It is not a statutory audit, tax audit, certification or assurance engagement unless a separate engagement is specifically agreed with an appropriately qualified professional.

The checkup provides a broad view of the business and helps identify areas that deserve attention. A diagnostic is a deeper, more focused examination of selected issues to understand their underlying causes, business impact and possible corrective measures.

The output depends on the agreed scope. It may include a summary of observations, areas requiring attention and suggested priorities. The format and level of detail are confirmed before the engagement begins.

It can help surface possible constraints across areas such as cash flow, margins, operations, reporting and compliance. Where a concern needs deeper investigation, a focused diagnostic can be recommended rather than drawing conclusions from limited information.
