Zero-Penalty Compliance Strategy

Proactive Tax Governance Built for Indian Regulatory Rigor

India's tax ecosystem is intensely automated. With electronic invoicing, real-time GST reconciliation (GSTR-2B), Annual Information Statements (AIS), and algorithmic scrutiny notices, treating tax compliance as a year-end panic is a recipe for heavy penalties and blocked input credits.

BookMyRecords provides a disciplined, calendar-driven compliance framework. Our tax professionals monitor your transactions monthly, claim every legitimate input tax credit, compute quarterly advance taxes accurately, and defend your business against departmental scrutiny.

The BMR Tax Commitment

  • Rigorous ITC Reconciliation: Reconcile 100% of vendor invoices against GSTR-2B before filing GSTR-3B to eliminate reversal notices.
  • Proactive Advance Tax: Accurate quarterly forecasting that protects your liquidity and avoids punitive 234B/234C interest.
  • Notice Resolution Support: Seasoned CAs draft factual, law-backed submissions for Income Tax and GST intimations.

Frequently Asked Questions on Taxation

Practical compliance insights on GST input tax credits, corporate advance tax calculations, Section 44AB thresholds, and departmental scrutiny defense.

Under current GST rules, you cannot claim Input Tax Credit (ITC) unless the invoice is reflected in your auto-drafted GSTR-2B. We run monthly automated reconciliations comparing your purchase vouchers against GSTR-2B. If a supplier fails to upload invoices or files late, we flag the discrepancy immediately before GSTR-3B is filed, saving you from recovery notices and mandatory 18% interest.

Advance tax must be deposited in four installments: 15% by June 15, 45% by September 15, 75% by December 15, and 100% by March 15. If your estimated annual tax liability exceeds ₹10,000 and installments are delayed, mandatory penal interest is charged under Sections 234B (1% per month) and 234C (1% per month). We prepare quarterly computations to ensure exact dues are deposited on time.

Yes. Our chartered accountants regularly handle historical notices, rectification petitions, and appeal submissions. We inspect the notice details, review the underlying ledger entries and returns, draft a legally grounded submission, and upload all requisite documentary evidence to the official portal.

For NRIs selling property or repatriating rental income/dividends from India, commercial banks require a CA certification on Form 15CB and an online Form 15CA declaration. We compute capital gains, claim deductions (e.g., Section 54/54EC), apply Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement (DTAA) treaty rates, and issue certified documentation for prompt bank processing.

For business entities with digital receipts exceeding 95% of gross turnover, the tax audit threshold is ₹10 Crores. For professionals, the audit threshold is ₹50 Lakhs (or ₹75 Lakhs under presumptive Section 44ADA if cash receipts are under 5%). We monitor your turnover throughout the year to ensure timely audit readiness.

Failure to deduct TDS attracts 1% interest per month from the date due until deduction. Failure to deposit deducted TDS attracts 1.5% interest per month until actual payment. Crucially, 30% of expenditure where TDS was not deducted/deposited is disallowed under Section 40(a)(ia), directly inflating taxable business profits.

At year-end, we perform an exhaustive three-way reconciliation matching your audited financial statements, general ledgers, GSTR-1, and GSTR-3B filings. For entities with turnover exceeding ₹5 Crores, we prepare the self-certified GSTR-9C reconciliation statement, correcting any tax differences through DRC-03.

Do not panic. Section 143(1) intimations usually highlight automated computational mismatches or TDS credit differences between your return and Form 26AS/AIS. We analyze the intimation, identify the exact reason for the demand or deduction mismatch, and file an online rectification under Section 154 or an updated response within 30 days.
