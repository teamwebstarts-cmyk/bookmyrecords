Professional Bookkeeping & Management Accounting

Clean, Timely Books Without the Overhead of an In-House Department

Managing an in-house accounting department is an ongoing administrative drain: unexpected staff turnover, knowledge gaps in GST changes, delayed bank reconciliations, and year-end audit panics. When transactions aren't entered promptly, business promoters fly blind without knowing their true cash position or profitability.

BookMyRecords provides seamless outsourced accounting on Tally Prime. Our trained accounting team handles your day-to-day vouchers, reconciles banking channels weekly, computes monthly P&L statements, and prepares flawless audit schedules under the supervision of qualified Chartered Accountants.

The BookMyRecords Assurance

  • Chartered Accountant Supervision: Every ledger, classification, and tax deduction is vetted by a qualified CA.
  • Weekly Reconciliation: Zero discrepancy between bank feeds, payment gateways, and accounting ledgers.
  • Year-End Audit Readiness: Full schedules ready for statutory auditors, saving weeks of year-end disruption.
