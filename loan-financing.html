Bank-Compliant Credit Documentation

Why Proper Financial Structuring Is Key to Bank Loan Approval

Commercial banks and credit institutions reject a high percentage of business loan applications not because the business lacks merit, but because submitted figures lack proper structure. Inconsistent projections, unverified historical ratios, missing fund-flow calculations, and haphazard balance sheets create immediate red flags for credit underwriting committees.

BookMyRecords prepares rigorous, bank-approved credit dossiers. We structure your historical audited financials, compute accurate Maximum Permissible Bank Finance (MPBF) calculations, draft bankable Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), and ensure your financial ratios satisfy lending criteria.

The BMR Documentation Standard

  • Rigorous Ratio Conformance: Current Ratio, Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR), Interest Service Coverage Ratio (ISCR), and Debt-Equity modeled to RBI and bank benchmark norms.
  • Multi-Year Forecasting: 2 to 3 years audited past data paired with realistic 3 to 5 year revenue and cash flow projections.
  • Underwriting Defense: We prepare promoters to answer credit manager questions regarding capacity utilization, debtor cycles, and inventory turnaround.

Credit Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions on Credit & Loans

Practical answers on bank credit underwriting, CMA reports, and MSME debt structuring.

Credit Monitoring Arrangement (CMA) Data is a standardized financial model required by public and private commercial banks to assess working capital limits (Cash Credit / Overdraft). It analyzes two years of audited financials, the current year’s estimated performance, and three to five years of projected operating statements, balance sheets, fund-flow statements, and key financial ratios (Current Ratio, DSCR, TOL/TNW).

The Credit Guarantee Fund Trust for Micro and Small Enterprises (CGTMSE) allows eligible MSMEs to secure business loans up to ₹5 Crores without pledging immovable property or third-party collateral. The Trust guarantees 75% to 85% of the loan amount to the lending bank, enabling viable enterprises to obtain funding based purely on cash-flow projections and business merit.

A Term Loan is a fixed capital disbursement repaid through scheduled monthly EMIs over 3 to 7 years, primarily used to finance machinery, equipment, factory construction, or commercial vehicles. A Cash Credit (CC) / Overdraft (OD) limit is a revolving working capital credit line pegged to your debtors and stock, where interest is charged only on the exact funds utilized on a daily basis.

Common rejection triggers include: (1) Unreconciled differences between GST sales returns and audited P&L turnover, (2) Poor Current Ratio (below 1.33:1) indicating liquidity stress, (3) High debtor days with aging receivables, (4) Unexplained unsecured loan borrowings from family/promoters, and (5) Flawed CMA projections that do not match industry benchmarks. We audit and scrub these vulnerabilities before submission.

DSCR measures your net operating income against total debt service obligations (principal + interest payments). Commercial banks typically look for an average DSCR of 1.50:1 to 2.00:1. A DSCR below 1.25:1 is considered high-risk and usually leads to loan rejection or demands for excessive collateral.

Under the Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP), eligible new manufacturing projects up to ₹50 Lakhs and service projects up to ₹20 Lakhs can receive government capital subsidies ranging between 15% and 35%, depending on urban/rural location and social category.
